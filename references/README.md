# References and related public work

## Mathematical sources

- Jorge A. Guccione, Juan J. Guccione, Rodrigo Horruitiner, and Christian Valqui, “Some algorithms related to the Jacobian Conjecture.” [arXiv:1708.07936](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.07936).
- Jorge Alberto Guccione, Juan José Guccione, Rodrigo Horruitiner, and Christian Valqui, “Increasing the degree of a possible counterexample to the Jacobian Conjecture from 100 to 108.” [arXiv:2204.14178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14178).
- S. S. Abhyankar, “Conics as Axes and Jacobian Problem,” *The Mathematics Student* 76 (2007), 171–183. [PDF](https://www.indianmathsoc.org/ms/mathstudent2007.pdf).
- S. S. Abhyankar, *Expansion Techniques in Algebraic Geometry*, TIFR Lectures on Mathematics and Physics 57 (1977). [TIFR PDF](https://mathweb.tifr.res.in/sites/default/files/publications/ln/tifr57.pdf).
- B. Helali, 2026 paper/report on the degree bound; check the deposited version and metadata before any formal citation. [Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21479814).

## Related computational projects

- Will Strinz, [plane-jacobian-75-125](https://github.com/wstrinz/plane-jacobian-75-125). This is an independent public project focused on the same degree pair. Its claims and proof boundaries belong to its own repository and manuscript.
- Roy van Rijn, [jacobian-research](https://github.com/royvanrijn/jacobian-research). An independent computational research repository containing related F2 calculations.

This archive does not vendor those repositories. Verify current versions, author names, and theorem scopes at the linked sources before relying on this bibliography in a publication.
